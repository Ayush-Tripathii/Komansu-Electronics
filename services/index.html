CCTV Surveillance

IP and analog camera systems, NVR/DVR infrastructure, video management, and storage planning for perimeter, indoor, and critical-area monitoring. We design systems from site survey to as-built documentation, covering industrial facilities, hospitality properties, and commercial campuses.

  • IP megapixel, PTZ, fisheye, and thermal cameras
  • Analog HD (HDCVI/TVI) for retrofit projects
  • NVR/DVR with RAID storage design
  • VMS integration: Milestone, Genetec, Hikvision, Dahua
  • AutoCAD layout drawings, cable schedules, and BOQ
  • Compliant with IEC 62676 and project security requirements

Intelligent Video Analytics

AI-driven analytics that transform passive CCTV infrastructure into an active security layer — reducing operator fatigue and delivering actionable alerts. Deployed at the edge or on a dedicated server, integrated with your existing cameras or as a standalone system.

  • Perimeter intrusion detection and virtual tripwire
  • Loitering, crowd density, and abandoned object alerts
  • Licence plate recognition (LPR/ANPR)
  • Face recognition and people counting
  • AI-based fire and smoke detection

03NFPA 72 · EN 54

Fire Alarm & PA Systems

Addressable and conventional Fire Alarm System (FAS) design, and Public Address & General Alarm (PAGA) for evacuation and area communication — engineered for industrial plants, petrochemical facilities, hotels, and commercial buildings. All designs comply with NFPA 72, BS 5839, and EN 54.

  • Addressable FAS: Notifier, Hochiki, Siemens, Apollo compatible
  • Loop-based panel design with point-by-point device mapping
  • PAGA: amplifier sizing, zone routing, speaker tapping calculations
  • Voice evacuation system (VES) per EN 54-16 and NFPA 72 Ch. 24
  • Integration with suppression systems, fire doors, and BMS
  • Single-line diagrams, zone schedules, and complete BOQ

04Card · Biometric

Access Control Systems

Card reader, biometric, and intercom-integrated solutions with centralised management — from single-entry offices to multi-gate industrial facilities. We design for security stringency and operational practicality, with full door hardware specification, cable segregation, and integration with HR and visitor management systems.

  • IP-based controllers: HID, Lenel, Honeywell, ZKTeco, Suprema
  • Card technologies: MIFARE, DESFire, HID iCLASS, proximity
  • Biometric: fingerprint, face recognition, and iris readers
  • Turnstiles, boom barriers, and vehicle access integration
  • Fail-safe vs. fail-secure door hardware specification
  • AutoCAD drawings, device schedules, and complete BOQ

05TIA-568 · ISO 11801

Data Networks & WiFi Infra

Structured cabling (Cat6/6A/Fibre), core and edge switching, wireless access point planning, and telecom room builds for offices, hotels, and industrial plant environments. Designed to TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801, coordinated with IT teams and main contractors for seamless integration.

  • Structured cabling: Cat6, Cat6A (copper), OS2/OM3/OM4 fibre
  • MDF/IDF design: rack layouts, power, patching schedules
  • Core and access switching: Cisco, HPE Aruba, Ubiquiti
  • Enterprise WiFi: site survey, AP placement, channel planning
  • VLAN design and network segmentation for OT/IT convergence
  • TIA-568-C and ISO/IEC 11801 compliant with test reports
